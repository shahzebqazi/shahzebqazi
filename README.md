<u><strong>I build at the intersection of rigor and runtime.</strong></u>  
My head lives in **category theory** and **complex analysis**—the kind of math that quietly decides whether your abstractions *earn their keep*. In code, that usually means **Haskell** and **functional programming** first, because I like systems that stay honest when everything else gets messy.

On the human side, I care about **UX/UI** and **HCI research**: interfaces aren’t “skin,” they’re *contracts* between minds and machines. On the machine side, I work on **AI harnesses**, **inference**, and **agents**—not as magic, but as *engineered behavior* with budgets, failure modes, and receipts.

I’m also a **game dev** (because play is the ultimate HCI lab) and a **DSP** person (because the world is continuous until you sample it).

*If it can be formalized, optimized, heard, or played—I’m probably already obsessing over it.*
